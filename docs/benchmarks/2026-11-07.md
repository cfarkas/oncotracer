# Resource scheduling benchmark — 7 November 2026

## Recorded phase results

These are measurements from the 2.3.2 scheduling candidate on 6 November,
retained as evidence for the scheduler carried into 3.0.0. They are not a new
3.0.0 full-pipeline speed measurement.

| Workload | Reference measurement | Parallel measurement | Output check |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Eight Clair3 chunks | 550.534 s, sum of individually measured chunk times | 83.062408 s, eight workers | Identical variant records |
| Two QUILT2 chr22 chunks | Existing completed chunk outputs | 39.018873 s, dynamic workers | 171,655 identical variant records |

The Clair3 ratio is **6.628× for these eight chunks**. The reference is the sum
of individual chunk timings, not a separate serial end-to-end study. It does not
predict a 6.628× speedup for ONT alignment, imputation, reporting, or the full study.
No QUILT2 speedup ratio is claimed without a comparable serial wall time.

## Study continuation

The 3.0.0 continuation uses saved outputs for 11 Illumina Fresh, 26 Illumina FFPE,
and 22 ONT Fresh samples. It measures validation and remaining work, not fresh
analysis from FASTQ. CPU limits are 22, 52, and 22 cores respectively; memory
limits are 176, 416, and 224 GiB. Dynamic workers stay within each study budget.

At the start of continuation, ONT small-variant analysis is complete. CNA quality
checks permit one of 22 samples at the selected 50 kb bin size. The other 21 remain
not assessable; GISTIC recurrence is skipped because fewer than two samples are
eligible. Reports must retain these limitations.

The [front page commands](https://github.com/cfarkas/oncotracer#recorded-study-commands) select compact
storage and dynamic resources explicitly. The original configurations and
measurements are retained locally at
`/media/server/storage_disk4/oncotracer_benchmark_07_11_2026`.
Public results contain aggregate measurements, without patient-level variants.

The 3.0.0 continuation measurements and final output checks are pending.
